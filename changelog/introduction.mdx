Welcome to the Camila changelog — the record of everything we ship to the platform. Every new feature, improvement, and fix lands here first, written so you can tell at a glance whether a change affects the way you work.

You'll also find that we like to organize our work in cycles and divide it by the subscription plans we offer:

- **Camila Pro** — a basic account for individual Real Estate agents.
- **Camila Business** — the full suite of features for small and mid-sized Real Estate companies.

## How we organize releases

### Cycles

We build in two-week cycles, the same ones our product and engineering teams run day to day. Each cycle has a number and a fixed date range, and every changelog page collects everything that shipped inside it. Cycle numbers are sequential and never reused, so Cycle 4 always points to the same two weeks of work.

### Subscription plans

Every entry carries the plan it ships on, so you never have to guess whether a change is available to your account:

| Plan | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Camila Pro** | Available on both Pro and Business accounts. |
| **Camila Business** | Exclusive to Business accounts. Pro users can upgrade at any time. |

<Note>
  Camila Business includes everything in Camila Pro, so an entry tagged **Camila Pro** is available to every customer.
</Note>

## How to read a new entry in the changelog

Each changelog entry follows a consistent anatomy so you can scan quickly and dig in only when relevant.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cycle and date">
    Entries are filed under the cycle they shipped in, with the exact release date — for example, `July 19th, 2026` — at the top of the entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Title">
    A concise summary of the change in 15 words or less, written from the perspective of what you can now do in Camila.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type">
    Every entry is one of **New feature**, **Feature improvement**, **New integration**, or **Bug fix**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Product category">
    The area of the product the change belongs to — for example **Leads**, **Property Hub**, **AI Workflows**, **Sales Knowledge**, or **Reports**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan availability">
    The subscription plan the change ships on — **Camila Pro** or **Camila Business**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What changed and why">
    A short explanation of what we built and the problem it solves for your sales operation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How to try it">
    The steps to find the change in production, so you can test it yourself right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>
