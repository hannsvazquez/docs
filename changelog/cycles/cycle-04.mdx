### July 19th, 2026 — Camila follows up cold leads with WhatsApp templates

**Feature improvement** · **AI Workflows** · **Camila Business**

Reactivating a lead who went quiet used to be manual work that agents rarely got around to. Camila now identifies the leads who left you on read or simply forgot to reply, adds them to a follow-up list, and sends a flow of WhatsApp templates personalized to the project each lead was interested in — with the images they need to reconnect.

Every outbound message also carries a live delivery status, so your agents know whether a follow-up was **Enviado**, **Entregado**, **Leído**, or **Fallido** without leaving the contact record. Follow-up messages sent by Camila display the template name in plain Spanish instead of the raw template key.

**How to try it:** Open any contact in the CRM and go to the **WhatsApp** tab. Messages sent by Camila show the template name and a colored delivery badge next to the timestamp.
