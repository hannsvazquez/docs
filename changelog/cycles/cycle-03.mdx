### June 30th, 2026 — Suspend and reactivate sales users from the team list

**New feature** · **Settings** · **Camila Business**

Administrators can now suspend a sales user directly from the team list and reactivate them later, without deleting the account or losing the history attached to it. This keeps your pipeline data intact when someone leaves the team temporarily or moves to another project.

**How to try it:** Go to **Settings → Sales** and open the actions menu on any team member to suspend or reactivate them.

---

### June 30th, 2026 — Search queries are now sanitized before they run

**Bug fix** · **Leads** · **Camila Pro**

Certain characters typed into the search bar could return an error instead of results. All search input is now sanitized before the query runs, so looking up names, phone numbers, or addresses that contain accents, quotes, or symbols returns results reliably.

**How to try it:** Search for a contact using a name or address that contains accents or special characters.
