## How this site is organized

Camila's release documentation is split into two spaces to serve different audiences:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Changelog (Technical)">
    The **Changelog** tab contains detailed, engineer-facing release notes. We publish one entry at the end of every two-week cycle, tagged by type, product category, and the subscription plan it ships on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Release (Public)">
    The **Press Release** tab is maintained by the Camila Product Marketing team. It's organized by app version and written for business users, decision-makers, and the broader Camila community.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay current">
    Bookmark this hub and check back after each release. The current cycle is always pinned at the top of the Changelog, and the latest version leads the Press Release.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Looking for a specific fix or feature? Use the search bar at the top of the page to find any entry by keyword, cycle number, or version.
</Note>
